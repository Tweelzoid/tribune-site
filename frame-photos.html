Your own photos on The Frame, from your iPhone

Samsung's SmartThings app is the official way to add your own pictures to The Frame's Art Mode. When it works, fine. When it says the upload failed, shows an empty photo picker, or can't find the TV, try these first.

If SmartThings won't upload

  1. Put the iPhone and the TV on the same Wi-Fi network, not a guest network.
  2. On the iPhone, open Settings › SmartThings and turn on Local Network. Then check Photos is set to Full Access, or that the photos you want are in the allowed set.
  3. Press the TV remote's power button once to leave Art Mode, try the upload again, and choose Allow if the TV asks.
  4. Still stuck: restart the TV by holding the remote's power button until it turns off and on.

A simpler way

Gazette is an iPhone app that sends pictures straight to The Frame over your home Wi-Fi. Pick a photo, tap Send, and it shows in Art Mode a few seconds later. Turn on Picture only to send just the photo, or leave it off to add a slim strip with your calendar, the weather and dinner.

  • Finds your TV on its own; no hub or account
  • Crops photos to fit the screen
  • Can update the TV every morning, even with the phone locked
  • Removes the pictures it sent before, so the TV doesn't fill up

Try it free with demo mode first, no TV needed.
