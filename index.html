Your day, on the painting on your wall

Gazette puts a painting, your calendar, the weather and tonight's dinner on your Samsung TV's Art Mode each morning. Everything is made on your iPhone and sent over your home Wi-Fi.

Skip the second screen

Family wall calendars are another screen to buy, mount, charge and subscribe to. If you have a Samsung TV with Art Mode, the biggest screen in the house is already on the wall.
