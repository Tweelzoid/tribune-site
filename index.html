Your day, on the painting on your wall

Tribune puts a painting, your calendar, the weather and tonight's dinner on your Samsung TV's Art Mode each morning. Everything is made on your iPhone and sent over your home Wi-Fi.

What you need

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later
  • A Samsung TV with Art Mode, such as The Frame, on the same Wi-Fi
