Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Gazette does not collect, store or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The developer never receives your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your calendar events, dinners, chosen paintings and settings are read and kept only on your iPhone. The daily screen is drawn on your iPhone and sent directly to your TV over your home Wi-Fi.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Weather. Your approximate location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. It is not linked to you.
  • Paintings. Searches you type in the painting library are sent to the Art Institute of Chicago's public collection service to find images.
  • App settings check. Gazette occasionally downloads a small settings file from this website. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled by Apple. The developer does not see your payment details.

Household sharing

If you turn on household sharing, the dinners and display settings are stored as events in a calendar you choose to share, through your own calendar provider such as iCloud. Only the people you invite can see them.

Your choices

You can turn off calendar and location access at any time in iPhone Settings › Gazette. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.

Children

Gazette is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions: in the app, open Activity and tap Send report, or see the help page.

Gazette is an independent app, not affiliated with Samsung.
