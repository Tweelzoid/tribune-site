Help

Most problems are fixed from the stethoscope button in the app, which checks each step and offers a fix.

The app can't find my TV

Check that the TV and iPhone are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network), and that iPhone Settings › Tribune › Local Network is on. You can also type the TV's address: on the TV, Settings › General › Network › Network Status › IP Settings.

The TV never asked me to allow the phone

Samsung can't show the prompt in Art Mode. Press the TV remote's power button once to leave Art Mode, tap Connect in the app, choose Allow on the TV, then press power again.

The morning update didn't happen

Open the Shortcuts app › Automation and check the 5:00 AM automation is set to Run Immediately with the Update Frame action. On the TV, turn on Settings › General › Network › Expert Settings › Power On with Mobile so the phone can wake it.

Which TVs work?

Samsung The Frame from 2020 onward is tested. Other Samsung TVs with Art Mode may work; try demo mode first, then a free trial.
