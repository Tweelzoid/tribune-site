Help

Most problems are fixed from the line at the top of the app: tap it to open Settings, where Health checks each step and offers a fix.

The app can't find my TV

Check that the TV and iPhone are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network), and that iPhone Settings › Gazette › Local Network is on. You can also type the TV's address: on the TV, Settings › General › Network › Network Status › IP Settings.

The TV never asked me to allow the phone

Samsung can't show the prompt in Art Mode. Press the TV remote's power button once to leave Art Mode, tap Connect in the app, choose Allow on the TV, then press power again.

The morning update didn't happen

Gazette updates the TV overnight while your iPhone is charging and on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. Check three things: Background App Refresh is on for Gazette (iPhone Settings › General › Background App Refresh), Low Power Mode is off overnight, and on the TV, Settings › General › Network › Expert Settings › Power On with Mobile is on so the phone can wake it.

Can I pick the exact time?

Yes, optionally. In the app, open Settings › Exact-time automation for a step-by-step guide to a Shortcuts automation (for example 5:00 AM, Run Immediately, Update Frame).

Which TVs work?

Samsung The Frame from 2020 onward is tested. Other Samsung TVs with Art Mode may work; try demo mode first, then a free trial.
